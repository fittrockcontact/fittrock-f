Sitting statically for 8+ hours a day places sustained mechanical stress on your lower lumbar vertebrae and restricts venous blood flow. Alternating between sitting and standing every 45 minutes reduces fatigue and increases cognitive focus.

## 1. The 90-Degree Elbow Angle Rule
When standing at your Fittrock electric desk, set your height preset so your forearms rest parallel to the desktop surface, forming a 90-to-100 degree bend at the elbow. Your wrists should remain completely neutral while typing, without hyperextending upwards or drooping downwards.

## 2. Monitor Eye Level Clearance
The top third of your monitors should align directly with your horizontal eye line. Having to tilt your neck up or down by even 15 degrees multiplies the effective weight of your head on the cervical spine from 5kg to over 12kg. Using a dynamic gas-spring monitor arm ensures instant elevation adjustment.

## 3. The 45:15 Standing-to-Sitting Ratio
Don't stand all day immediately! The golden ratio recommended by orthopedic experts is:
- **45 Minutes Sitting**: Engaged, supported by high-density lumbar mesh seating.
- **15 to 20 Minutes Standing**: Active upright posture with weight evenly distributed across both feet.

## 4. Footwear and Anti-Fatigue Mats
When standing on hard tile or hardwood floors, utilize an anti-fatigue polyurethane mat or supportive cushioned footwear to reduce joint compression in the knees and ankles.

## Summary
Incorporating motorized sit-stand elevation into your daily workflow boosts energy, burns up to 200 extra calories per day, and completely eliminates chronic afternoon back tightness.
