Nothing ruins the sleek aesthetic of a motorized standing desk faster than a rat's nest of dangling power cables and adapter bricks. Here are 5 essential cable management strategies to achieve an immaculate setup.

## 1. Mount an Under-Desk Heavy-Duty Cable Tray
The cornerstone of any clean setup is a spacious under-desk metal tray. Mount it toward the rear frame of your desk to house large power strips, laptop charging bricks, and surge protectors out of sight.

## 2. Implement a Flexible Articulating Cable Spine
Because standing desks elevate and lower continuously, you need a flexible vertical spine that guides all master power cords from the desk down to your wall socket without pinching or tangling.

## 3. Route Monitor Cables Through Gas-Spring Arms
Modern monitor arms include internal cable channel clips. Route your HDMI, DisplayPort, and power cables through these channels to make your floating screens appear completely wireless.

## 4. Utilize Velcro Cable Ties Instead of Plastic Zip Ties
Velcro wraps allow you to add or remove peripheral cables in seconds without needing scissors or risking wire cuts.

## 5. Single Thunderbolt / USB-C Docking
Reduce multiple laptop cables to a single USB-C cable that provides power delivery, external display output, and peripheral connectivity simultaneously.
