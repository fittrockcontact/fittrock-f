When shopping for an electric height adjustable standing desk, the most crucial architectural distinction is whether the frame is powered by a single central motor with a connecting hex driveshaft, or dual synchronized motors integrated directly into each telescopic leg.

## 1. Lifting Capacity & Load Balance
- **Single Motor Desks**: Maximize around 70kg to 80kg capacity. Uneven weight distribution (e.g. heavy dual monitors on one side) can cause mechanical binding over time.
- **Dual Motor Desks (KUBER Series)**: Provide heavy-duty 125kg load capacity. Each leg independently pushes its own weight, ensuring zero motor strain even with multiple heavy ultrawide monitors and tower PCs.

## 2. Speed and Noise Levels
Dual motors share the mechanical load evenly, allowing the frame to elevate at 35mm/second with whisper-quiet operation under 45 decibels—silent enough that colleagues won't hear transitions during microphone calls.

## 3. Anti-Collision Gyro Sensitivity
Fittrock dual motor desks feature real-time six-axis gyro sensors that instantly reverse the motor direction if the tabletop detects an obstruction like under-desk armrests or power banks.

## Conclusion
If you run a minimal single-laptop setup, a single motor desk like the **Fittrock RATI** offers unbeatable value. For multi-monitor power users, the **Fittrock KUBER Dual Motor** is the gold standard for lifetime durability.
